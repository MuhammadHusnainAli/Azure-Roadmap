# 32 · IoT, Industry and Specialised Services

> Tracks: E – A ○ C ○ · Part E · Secure, Operate, Migrate and Extend

Beyond general-purpose compute, data and AI, Azure offers services for connected devices, specific industries and specialised domains. This topic covers Azure IoT Hub and the Device Provisioning Service; Azure IoT Operations (edge data plane on Arc-enabled Kubernetes) and IoT Edge (1.6 LTS; 1.5 support ends 10 November 2026); Azure Digital Twins and Fabric's digital twin builder; healthcare with Azure Health Data Services (Azure API for FHIR deprecated 30 September 2026), the healthcare agent service and healthcare AI models; financial services, retail and manufacturing patterns and regulations; energy (Azure Data Manager for Energy), sustainability and geospatial (Planetary Computer Pro, Azure Maps, carbon optimisation); Azure Quantum at awareness level; and Microsoft Marketplace and Azure Native Integrations.

## What you will be able to do

- Design device connectivity and provisioning at scale.
- Choose between IoT Hub, IoT Operations and IoT Edge for industrial scenarios.
- Model environments with Digital Twins.
- Build healthcare solutions on FHIR/DICOM with appropriate AI and safeguards.
- Apply industry regulations and patterns in finance, retail and manufacturing.
- Recognise when energy, geospatial and sustainability services fit.
- Explain Azure Quantum and post-quantum readiness.
- Procure and govern partner solutions through Microsoft Marketplace.
