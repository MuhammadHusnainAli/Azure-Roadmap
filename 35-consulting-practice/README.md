# 35 · Consulting Practice

> Tracks: E – A ○ C ● · Part F · Architecture, Consulting and Career

This topic covers the consulting craft around Azure and AI: the consultant role and engagement types; discovery and envisioning workshops; use-case prioritisation and business cases; AI readiness assessments; moving from proof of concept to production without pilot purgatory; proposals, estimation and statements of work; solution design deliverables (HLD, LLD, decision logs, handover packs); stakeholder communication and explaining AI risk plainly; change management and adoption; licensing and commercials (Azure agreements, Copilot, Agent 365, Security Copilot, partner programmes); and delivery governance for AI projects.

## What you will be able to do

- Position and run the right engagement for a client's situation.
- Facilitate discovery and envisioning that produce a prioritised, owned backlog.
- Build credible business cases with realistic benefits.
- Assess AI readiness and produce an evidence-based roadmap.
- Scope PoCs and pilots with exit criteria and a production path.
- Write proposals, estimates and SoWs that handle AI uncertainty.
- Produce design and handover deliverables people use.
- Communicate with executives, handle objections and drive adoption.
- Navigate Microsoft licensing and programmes and govern delivery.

Broad familiarity with Parts A–E, especially [Topic 22 file 10](../22-evaluation-observability-and-genaiops/10-measuring-business-value.md), [Topic 24](../24-copilot-and-low-code-agents/README.md), [Topic 29](../29-cost-management-and-finops/README.md) and [Topic 33](../33-solution-architecture/README.md).
