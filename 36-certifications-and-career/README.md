# 36 · Certifications and Career

> Tracks: E ● A ● C ● · Part F · Architecture, Consulting and Career

Microsoft changed its Azure and AI exam codes substantially in 2026:
- AI-900, AI-102, DP-100, AZ-204 and AZ-500 retired.
- AI-901, AI-103, AI-300, AI-200 and SC-500 replaced them.
- AB-100 became the architect-level credential for agentic AI business solutions.

This topic maps each certification relevant to the roadmap to its skills measured and to the roadmap topics that prepare you for it. It finishes with portfolio, interview and career guidance. **Exam details change often: confirm status on Microsoft Learn before booking.**

## What you will be able to do

- Explain what retired in 2026, what replaced it, and how renewal and Applied Skills work.
- Choose a certification path for the engineer, architect or consultant track.
- Follow a study plan mapped to roadmap topics for each core exam.
- Pick an adjacent security, data or DevOps certification that removes delivery blockers.
- Build a portfolio and prepare for interviews in your track.

## Prerequisites

None for reading. Each exam file lists the roadmap topics to study first.

## Suggested certification paths

|---|---|

| AI Solution Architect | (AZ-900) → AZ-104 → AI-103 → AZ-305 → AB-100 |
| Azure Consultant | AZ-900 → AI-901 → AI-103 or AZ-104 → AB-100 or AZ-305 |
