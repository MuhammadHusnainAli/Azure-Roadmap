# Certification Landscape

## Why it matters

Microsoft rebuilt much of its Azure and AI certification portfolio in 2026. Long-standing exams such as AI-900, AI-102, AZ-204, DP-100 and AZ-500 retired, and new exams built around Microsoft Foundry, agents and AI operations replaced them. If you follow a study plan or a job advert written in 2025, you may prepare for an exam that no longer exists. This file maps what retired, what replaced it, how the credential types differ and how renewal works, so you can plan a certification path that matches the three roles in this roadmap.

Exam details change often. **Always confirm the current status on the exam page and study guide on Microsoft Learn before booking.**

## Key concepts

### Credential types

| Type | What it proves | Format | Expires? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fundamentals** | Concepts and basic hands-on familiarity | Proctored exam | No |
| **Associate (role-based)** | Ability to perform a job role | Proctored exam | Yes, annually; free online renewal |
| **Expert (role-based)** | Advanced design or engineering for a role | Proctored exam plus a prerequisite Associate certification | Yes, annually; free online renewal |
| **Specialty** | Narrow product depth | Proctored exam | Yes, annually |
| **Applied Skills** | One real-world scenario | Interactive lab-based assessment | Check the credential page |

### What changed in 2026 (as listed on Microsoft Learn)

| Retired exam | Retirement date | Replacement |
|---|---|---|
| **AI-900** Azure AI Fundamentals | 30 June 2026 | **AI-901** Microsoft Azure AI Fundamentals (same certification name) |
| **AI-102** Azure AI Engineer Associate | 30 June 2026 | **AI-103** Azure AI Apps and Agents Developer Associate |
| **DP-100** Azure Data Scientist Associate | 1 June 2026 | **AI-300** Machine Learning Operations Engineer Associate |
| **AZ-204** Azure Developer Associate | 31 July 2026 | **AI-200** Azure AI Cloud Developer Associate |
| **AZ-500** Azure Security Engineer Associate | 31 August 2026 | **SC-500** Cloud and AI Security Engineer Associate (available from 21 July 2026) |
| **PL-200** Power Platform Functional Consultant | 31 August 2026 | **AB-410** Intelligent Applications Builder Associate |
| **PL-600** Power Platform Solution Architect Expert | 30 June 2026 | AB-100 is the closest agentic architect path (check page) |
| **AZ-800 / AZ-801** Windows Server Hybrid Administrator | 30 September 2026 | No direct replacement listed; see AZ-104 and Arc content |

Several Applied Skills retired as well, including *Create agents in Microsoft Copilot Studio*, *Implement knowledge mining with Azure AI Search* and *Develop generative AI apps with Azure OpenAI and Semantic Kernel*.

Microsoft states that partners keep credit for retired certifications for one year after the retirement date. Once a certification retires you cannot renew it, and there's no automatic conversion to the replacement: you sit the new exam.

### Exams that remain current (at time of writing)

AZ-900, AZ-104, AZ-305, AZ-400, AZ-700, SC-100, SC-200, SC-300, DP-600, DP-700 and AB-100. Several were updated during 2026 (for example AZ-305 on 17 April 2026, AZ-400 and AZ-700 on 27 July 2026, SC-100 on 21 October 2026, and AB-100 on 16 November 2026), so use the current study guide.

### Renewal

- Associate, Expert and Specialty certifications are valid for one year.
- Renewal is **free**: an unproctored, open-book online assessment on Microsoft Learn.
- The renewal window opens six months before expiry; passing extends the certification by one year from the expiry date.
- Fundamentals certifications don't expire.

### Applied Skills

Applied Skills are lab-based assessments of a single scenario, such as deploying a specific solution. They suit consultants who want to show a narrow, practical skill quickly, or who want a stepping stone before a role-based exam. The catalogue changes often; several 2025-era AI Applied Skills retired in 2026.

## How it works on Azure

### Roadmap tracks to certifications

| Track | Foundation | Core | Advanced |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Azure AI Engineer (E)** | AZ-900, AI-901 | AI-103, AI-200 | AI-300 |
| **AI Solution Architect (A)** | AZ-900, AI-901 | AZ-104, AI-103 | AZ-305, AB-100 |
| **Azure Consultant (C)** | AZ-900, AI-901 | AZ-104 or AI-103 | AZ-305, AB-100, SC-100 (security-led) |

### Booking and preparation

1. Read the exam page: languages, retirement date and the "updated on" notice.
2. Download the study guide and compare its *skills measured* date with your learning material.
3. Try the exam sandbox to learn the question formats.
4. Take the practice assessment (now hosted on AI Skills Navigator for many exams).
5. Book with a **personal Microsoft account**. Microsoft warns that records tied to a work account can be lost when you leave the organisation.

Most role-based exams have a passing score of 700. Microsoft says that most questions cover GA features, but commonly used Preview features can appear.

## Design decisions and trade-offs

| Decision | Guidance |
|---|---|
| **Fundamentals first?** | Optional for experienced engineers; useful for career changers and for consultants who need breadth fast. |
| **Associate before Expert** | Required: AZ-305 needs AZ-104, AB-100 needs one of several Associates, and SC-100 needs SC-200, SC-300 or SC-500. |
| **Breadth vs depth** | Pick one deep path (for example AI-103 then AI-300) before collecting many fundamentals. |
| **Certification vs portfolio** | Certifications get you shortlisted; projects and stories win interviews (file 11). |
| **Timing around updates** | When a study guide announces an update date, decide which version you'll sit and study that one. |

## Hands-on

1. Create or link your Microsoft Learn profile with a personal account.
2. For each target exam, record the exam page, study guide date, retirement date and prerequisites.
3. Build a 6–12-month certification plan for your track.
4. Try one exam sandbox.
5. Set a reminder for renewal windows for any certification you hold.

## Security, cost and reliability notes

- **Cost:** exam fees vary by country; renewals are free. Ask your employer or partner programme about vouchers.
- **Records:** use a personal account so your credentials move with you.
- **Credibility:** list exam codes *and* certification names on your CV; retired names stay valid on your transcript but you should explain them.

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Studying for AI-102 or AZ-204 in late 2026 | Check the retirement list; study AI-103 or AI-200 |
| Using outdated courses | Match the course to the study guide's skills-measured date |
| Missing a prerequisite for an Expert certification | Check the certification prerequisites section |
| Letting certifications expire | Renew during the six-month window |
| Booking with a work account | Use a personal Microsoft account |
